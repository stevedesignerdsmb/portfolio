Questions owners ask

Can SMB make ChatGPT recommend my business?

No one can guarantee what an assistant says. SMB shows what the assistants answer today, which missing or wrong facts are behind those answers, and helps you fix them. The re-check every two weeks shows which answers changed.

Does SMB change my website or Google profile?

Not by itself. SMB drafts the copy, markup and review replies. You review each one and publish it yourself, or approve SMB to publish where you’ve connected a tool that allows it.

What do I need to start?

Your business on Google Maps, or your website address. Connecting Search Console is optional and read-only.

Which assistants does it check?

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, with the same twelve questions each time so you can compare answers over time.

Questions owners ask

Does a match mean they’ll buy from me?

No. A match means a business looks like your best customers, not that it’s looking for what you sell. That’s why SMB shows the reason for each match and asks you to rate them before anyone gets an email.

Where does the business data come from?

Public registrations, business websites and SMB’s own estimates. Each match shows when its data was last read, and which values, like staff size, are estimates.

Will SMB contact these businesses?

No. If you connect Gmail, SMB writes drafts in your voice and leaves them in your drafts folder. You decide what to send.

What if I don’t use a CRM?

Use your sales or accounting tool, or upload a spreadsheet with your customers’ names and cities. More than 140 tools work with SMB, including Pipedrive, Attio and Close.
